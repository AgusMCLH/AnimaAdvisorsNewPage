TAX RESIDENCY

Tax residency in Uruguay, Paraguay and Panama for individuals and families

If your activity and wealth already cross borders, tax residency defines how much you pay and where. We handle it end to end.

Many families and executives still pay taxes where they no longer live, or choose a country for its climate and later discover its tax regime doesn't fit their wealth structure.

We assess your specific case, compare jurisdictions, and manage the tax residency transfer in an orderly way, with ongoing tax follow-up afterward.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the tax residency process take?

The timeline depends on the regime chosen and the completeness of the documentation submitted. In Paraguay the initial process tends to be resolved faster; in Uruguay there are more administrative stages, but the outcome is more stable over time.

Can I keep my original citizenship if I settle in Uruguay, Paraguay or Panama?

Yes. Tax residency is independent of citizenship: you keep your original passport and only change where you pay taxes based on your new tax domicile.

Does tax residency automatically grant legal or immigration residency?

Not necessarily. These are separate processes that can be handled in parallel or independently; we assess this for your specific case during the initial diagnosis.
