CORPORATE SERVICES

Offshore company formation in the BVI, Panama, Bahamas, and Nevis

Choosing the right jurisdiction defines your cost, your confidentiality, and your banking reputation before international financial institutions.

Many companies set up an offshore entity without comparing jurisdictions, only to discover later that the chosen registered agent does not respond, or that the structure does not withstand the scrutiny of a correspondent bank.

We evaluate your operation, select the appropriate jurisdiction, and manage the full incorporation process, including changing the registered agent when the structure already exists but is not functioning properly.

THE SERVICE

What our support includes

01

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between incorporating in the BVI or in Nevis?

BVI and Nevis offer greater corporate confidentiality and lower maintenance costs. The choice depends on how you plan to use the structure.

Do I need to travel to incorporate my offshore company?

In most cases, no. We act under power of attorney and local representation, so the entire process can be handled remotely, except for specific exceptions in certain jurisdictions.

What is a registered agent and why is it mandatory?

It is the authorized local entity that receives official notifications for your company from the jurisdiction's government. Every offshore company must maintain an active registered agent to remain in good legal standing.
